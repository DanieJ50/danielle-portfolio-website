→ QUEEN VESPERA NIGHTBLOOM

The working name evolved as the character became larger than a berry-themed antagonist. The final Queen Vespera Nightbloom is a boss queen with a sharper fantasy identity, her own region — Brambleveil Citadel — and the Eclipse Bloom power system.

03

PRODUCT CASE STUDY

SAVESIP / CUP$I

A gamified drink-spending tracker designed to make small everyday purchases visible without making budgeting feel cold or spreadsheet-heavy.

UX/UIBrandingGamificationProduct thinking
PROBLEM

Small café purchases feel harmless individually, but repeated habits add up.

PIVOT

Cup$i began as a clever money-forward symbol. SaveSip became warmer, friendlier, and easier to grow into a habit system.

SYSTEM

Drink logging, home-vs-store comparison, moods, challenges, levels, savings feedback, mascots, and a social recipe layer.
